# Finance Research
Determine required instrument, venue, quote currency and time range. Distinguish spot price, volume, derivatives open interest, funding and on-chain metrics. Record source timestamps and units.
Do not present stale or missing data as current. If no live tool is available, clearly state that and give a methodology rather than invented values. Cross-check material numbers and distinguish observed facts from interpretation. Explain uncertainty and avoid deterministic trading predictions or unsolicited execution.
